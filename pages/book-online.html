Important Information

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before your session. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session may incur a charge.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable athletic clothing
  • Athletic shoes appropriate for your sport
  • Water bottle
  • Optional: Personal protective equipment

Health & Safety

All participants must be in good health. If you have recent injuries or health concerns, please consult with a doctor before booking.

Arrival Instructions

Please arrive 15 minutes early for your first session to complete registration and meet your coach. Parking instructions will be sent in confirmation email.
