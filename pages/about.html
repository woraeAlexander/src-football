About PENTECOST UNIVERSITY SRC Sports Academy

Building Excellence, Promoting Community, Inspiring Champions

🎯 Our Mission

To provide world-class sports training and development programs that unlock the potential of athletes and foster a culture of excellence, teamwork, and personal growth within our university and surrounding community.

👁️ Our Vision

To become a leading regional sports institution recognized for producing competitive athletes, fostering community engagement, and establishing sustainable partnerships that advance sports development and academic excellence.

💎 Our Values

  • Excellence: Pursuit of highest standards
  • Integrity: Honesty and fairness
  • Community: Collective growth
  • Inclusion: Welcome all levels

Our Story

The Student Representative Council (SRC) Sports Academy was established with a vision to revolutionize sports training and development within our university. Recognizing the potential of talented athletes and the need for comprehensive sports programs, the SRC leadership secured funding and launched this initiative.

What started as a small initiative has grown into a thriving sports hub offering diverse programs from traditional favorites like football and rugby to emerging disciplines like taekwondo and wrestling.

Today, we continue to expand our offerings, invest in world-class facilities, and attract expert coaches dedicated to developing the next generation of athletes and leaders.
